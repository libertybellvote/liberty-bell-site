The Bell framework

Nine checks before we make a call.

No single viral clip, poll, or market price gets to decide the story.

01 / 18%

Polling

What do polling averages and presidential approval say? Where do they disagree with markets, and is that gap growing or shrinking?

02 / 12%

Betting odds

Fresh Polymarket and Kalshi prices. Each platform stays separately labeled. We never blend them into one mystery number.

03 / 12%

Campaign fundamentals

Fundraising, organization, endorsements, and real sourced numbers. Not vibes.

04 / 10%

Candidate quality

Communication under pressure, interviews, debates, and what actually happened on camera.

05 / 12%

Coalition strength

Is the base durable or an inch deep? Is it consolidating or starting to crack?

06 / 6%

Social sentiment

Real virality and organic attention. Attention is noted, but it is not confused with support.

07 / 10%

Momentum

The direction of travel, separate from the current level. Rising from low and falling from high tell different stories.

08 / 14%

Economic backdrop

Presidential approval, inflation, jobs, income, and voter pressure at the kitchen table. This is the strongest broad reality check.

09 / 6%

Fragility and insurgency

The single point of failure. Insider or outsider? Has that position changed?

The rule

Price is a signal. The nine checks decide the call.

Odds tell us what traders believe right now. The Bell asks whether polling, politics, organization, and the national environment support that belief.
